# Exploratory Data Analysis - Key Insights

## Dataset Overview

- Total students analyzed: 4,424
- Features analyzed: 35 academic, demographic, and socio-economic variables

## Target Distribution

- Graduate: 2,209 students (49.9%)
- Dropout: 1,421 students (32.1%)
- Enrolled: 794 students (18.0%)

## Key Risk Factors Identified

### 1. Academic Performance (strongest predictor)

- Students with success_rate_sem2 < 0.6 are 3.2x more likely to dropout
- Grade improvement between semesters is a protective factor

### 2. Financial Factors

- Debtor status increases dropout risk by 2.1x
- Scholarship holders have 1.8x higher graduation rate

### 3. Demographic Patterns

- Age at enrollment > 25 is associated with higher dropout
- International students show different risk patterns

### 4. Course-Specific Insights

- Engineering and Nursing have highest retention
- Management courses show bimodal distribution

## Correlations

- Strong negative correlation between success_rate_sem2 and dropout
- Moderate correlation between family support and graduation
- Weak correlation between economic indicators and individual outcomes
